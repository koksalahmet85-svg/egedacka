# Roadmap: Teraslar (Sözleşmenin Anatomisi)

Status: APPROVED on 2026-11-05 · Profile: Balanced · Format: 3:30, 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps

## 1. Concept
- **Logline:** A club's payroll is water released from a sluice at the top of a terraced hillside cut in section; three regulators carve three different geometries into the same slope, and where the water spills is where each rule bites.
- **The turn:** Before: a salary cap looks like one ceiling. After: three rules are three shapes (a ratio line that moves with revenue, a tax terrace whose overflow is shared, a fence of each club's own height).
- **Ending:** the camera pulls back to the three hillsides side by side, the same pour of water lands three ways; the opening sluice closes and the bamboo clapper tips one last time. Last question on screen: which slope would your club survive?
- **How code shows it:** a three.js diorama in cross section (Kesit Çizimi): hillside as stacked, flat-shaded terraces on cream paper, ink outlines; water as stylised extruded ribbons and droplets; numbers drawn as dimension lines and counters on the section.
- **Specificity test (writing.md §12):** (1) UEFA ratio steps 90→80→70 by calendar year; (2) squad cost includes amortisation and agent fees; (3) revenue includes player-sale profit; (4) Strasbourg 13 m€ + 12 m€ conditional; (5) Aston Villa 22,5 m€ and List A restriction; (6) EuroLeague pool 5.438.684 € from four clubs; (7) 362.579 € to each of 15; (8) Olympiacos paid exactly half its 606.580 € excess; (9) ASVEL under the floor paid its own players; (10) TFF limits from 9,0 bn TL (Galatasaray) down to 0,57 bn TL (Alanyaspor); (11) a TFF limit can be raised on application. **Concept:** maaş su, kural yamaç; her kurum suyu başka bir eğimle tutar. **Swap test:** moved to an NBA cap explainer, the 70% revenue line, the shared overflow cloud and the per-club fence no longer fit.

**Tempo:** standard ★ (a lecture film with numbers needs reading time) · average shot 6 s · shots ~35 · BPM 92

## 3. Look
- **Base:** custom, direction board row C "Kesit Çizimi".
- **Changes:** cream paper ground #f5ebdb, ink #2a2017, earth #825c2d / #735a56, sage #b4a389; water the one cool accent #3f7f8c (so money is always what the eye follows); flat shading, ink outlines, paper grain; frontal symmetric miniature framing.
- **References used:** research/board-v1/sheet.jpg (Moonrise Kingdom, Isle of Dogs: miniature, frontal, flat; Bali, Sa Pa, Banaue terraces: the shape of the walls; sluice gates: the wooden mechanism). Mood only.

## 4. Sound identity
- **Chosen concept:** Su ve ahşap.
- **Palette:** synthesised water (filtered noise, droplets), wood knocks, bamboo clapper, paper; a soft marimba-and-bass code score.
- **Signature sound:** the bamboo clapper fills through each act and tips with a 'tok' when a limit is crossed (S10, S16, S22 variant, S27).
- **Silence:** 0.8 s before the first tok; 1 s at the end.
- **Voice · music:** Turkish narrator, warm and curious, ~120 words a minute (~420 words) · code score, marimba, 92 BPM, one motif per regulator.
- **Ambience · shared reverb:** one paper-room bed; one small wooden room reverb.

## 5. Hook (first 2 s)
The sluice lifts on frame 1 and water hits the first terrace by 0:01; creak and splash start with it.

## 7. Director card
- **Structure:** a question in act one (where does the water stop?), answered three ways in three acts, compared in the coda.
- **Look:** Kesit Çizimi (user): section drawings make limits readable as lines. · **Camera:** calm miniature camera, frontal and symmetric; signature move: a vertical tilt down the section from catchment to spill. · **Motion:** "dolup taşan" (slow fill, quick spill, settle).
- **Tempo:** standard, 6 s average, ~35 shots. · **Transitions:** cuts; at each act a water wipe running down the section (once per act). · **Sound:** Su ve ahşap; narrator treatment chosen by ear at step 5.
- **Light:** one soft top light like a drafting lamp; slightly warmer in Act 4, neutral in the coda.
- **Signature:** the bamboo clapper and the opening sluice, returning in the coda.
- **How each thing is seen:** terraces as stepped section slices with a hatched cut face; water as a clear ribbon catching one highlight; clubs as engraved labels; money only as water and numbers, never coins or banknotes.
- **Atmosphere:** one place, a drafting-table diorama: near (ink labels), middle (terraces), far (paper horizon).
- **Motion design:** dimension lines (measure a limit), counters (split and share a sum), a moving ratio line (a rule tightening), labels that draw on.
- **Type:** at step 4b · **Texture:** paper grain, light ink wobble · **Finishing:** at the style frame.
