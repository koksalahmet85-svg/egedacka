# Script: Teraslar (Sözleşmenin Anatomisi)

Status: LOCKED on 2026-11-05 · Version: 2 · Runtime: ~210 s · Voice: Turkish narrator, warm and curious, calm pace ~2.2 words/s

## Angle
- **Logline:** A club's payroll is water released from a sluice at the top of a terraced hillside; UEFA, EuroLeague and TFF carve three different shapes into the same slope, and where the water spills is where each rule bites.
- **Why this angle:** students expect one "salary cap"; the film shows three geometries they can see: a line that moves with revenue, a wall whose overflow feeds rivals, a fence of each club's own height.
- **The turn:** Before: a cap is one ceiling. / After: three rules hold the same water three ways.
- **Ending:** three hillsides side by side, one pour; the first sluice closes, the clapper tips a last time; "Aynı su, üç yamaç."
