# Cold read: script-lines.md

Read: `review/script-lines.md` only (narration L2-L48, on-screen text L51-L67). Line numbers below are file line numbers in that file. I knew nothing about the film's idea beforehand.

## 1. What happened, in order (as a first-time viewer understands it)

1. **L2-L6.** A player signs. A "kapak" (hatch or sluice) opens at the club and money starts to flow. It has three parts: fixed salary (steady every month), bonuses (waves that come with goals and wins) and the signing fee (one bucket, poured once). On screen: sabit maaş / bonus / imza parası.
2. **L7-L9.** Every contract in the squad pours water onto "the slope". The question: where does the water have to stop? Three bodies in Europe answer it in three different ways.
3. **L10-L16, UEFA.** UEFA measures the rain that falls on the slope (club revenue, which includes profit from player sales) and the water that is held (squad cost: wages, transfer amortisation, agent fees). Squad cost may not go over 70% of revenue. The limit came down over three years, 90, 80, 70 (on screen: 2023, 2024, 2025), and 2025 was the first year measured at 70%.
4. **L17-L23.** The fines arrived in June 2026. Strasbourg: 13 m€, plus 12 m€ more if it does not cut spending. Aston Villa: 22.5 m€. Both had their European squads cut. Some clubs went over the line and were not fined, because "football profits" fully balanced the excess.
5. **L24-L34, EuroLeague.** EuroLeague builds a wall instead of a line, called the "taban ücret seviyesi". The three most expensive players do not count ("anchors"). If the water goes over the wall, part of the excess "evaporates": Olympiacos went 606,580 € over and paid exactly half, 303,290 €. In 2025-26 four clubs went over, and 5,438,684 € collected in the "cloud". It rained down on the 15 clubs that kept to the rule, 362,579 € each. Whoever goes over the wall waters its rivals.
6. **L35-L37.** The wall also has a lower limit. ASVEL stayed below it and had to pay the difference to its own players.
7. **L38-L46, TFF.** In Turkey, the TFF draws a separate fence for each club. The 2026-27 limits came out in June: Galatasaray 9 bn TL, Fenerbahçe 6.3 bn, Beşiktaş 4.1 bn, and the lowest, Alanyaspor, 571 m. Each club's fence is its own height. The fence also has a gate: if the club gives a reason, "the board" can raise the limit.
8. **L47-L48.** Same water, three slopes. One holds it with a ratio, one with a tax, one with a fence. On screen: Oran · Vergi · Çit.

**Ending premise (L47-L48):** "Oran" maps to UEFA's 70% (L14), which was never called a ratio but can be inferred. "Vergi" maps to the evaporation in L27, but the word "tax" never appears before the last line. "Çit" was set up in L38. So the ending mostly lands, but two of its three words are new vocabulary.
